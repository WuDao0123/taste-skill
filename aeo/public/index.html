FAQ

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend us?

No one can. AI answers change daily. We commit to concrete deliverables every month and measure your share of answers against named competitors, so you can see whether it is working.

How long until we see results?

Technical fixes are picked up within weeks. Content and third-party citations usually take 2 to 4 months, which is why monthly plans start with a 3-month term.

What does the free audit check?

Whether 8 major AI crawlers may read your site, whether you publish /llms.txt, structured data describing your product, comparison and alternatives pages, a public pricing page, FAQ content and basic page metadata. The paid audit adds what the AI engines actually answer.

Who is this for?

Bootstrapped and indie B2B SaaS, roughly $20k to $500k in monthly revenue, without an in-house SEO team.
