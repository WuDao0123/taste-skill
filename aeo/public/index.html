FAQ

What does the audit check?

Whether 8 major AI crawlers may read the site, /llms.txt, structured data describing the business, comparison and alternatives pages, a public pricing page, FAQ content and basic page metadata. Each item gets a score and a plain-English fix your team can sell.

How do I add it to my website?

Paste two lines of HTML where the audit should appear. It renders inside its own container, so it will not clash with your theme.

Where do the leads go?

Each visitor enters their email to see the report. You get an email for every new lead, can export them as CSV, and on Agency Pro can send them to Zapier or any webhook.

What happens if I hit the monthly limit?

The widget tells visitors to contact you instead of running the check. Upgrade from your dashboard to lift the limit straight away.
